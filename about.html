About Pi Trust Protocol

Pi Trust Protocol is the new address for secure trading in the Pi Network ecosystem. We bridge the trust gap between buyers and sellers globally.

How It Works

1. Deposit & Lock: The buyer initiates a trade and locks the required Pi amount into our decentralized secure escrow vault.
2. Verification & Delivery: The seller sees the secured Pi in the vault and confidently ships the product or delivers the service.
3. Approval & Release: Once the buyer receives and verifies the item, they hit "Release" and the Pi is safely transferred to the seller.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate scams and P2P fraud within the Pi Network. By offering a robust smart-contract style escrow platform, we ensure that every Pioneer can trade goods, services, and value with 100% peace of mind.
